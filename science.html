The one thing worth knowing

Sound travels about a hundred times faster than the gas does. So you always hear a fart before you smell it — usually by a few seconds.

Those few seconds are the only warning anyone gets. Which is exactly why the silent ones are the dangerous ones. They turn the warning off.
