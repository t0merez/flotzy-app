Most of it isn't you. It's furniture.

Only a couple of the eleven tricks below have anything to do with your body. The rest are about where you're sitting and what the room is made of. The same fart can be twice as loud on a wooden bench as on a sofa.

The single biggest one is a bucket. Genuinely.

The bucket calculator

Blow across the top of a bottle and it makes a note. Every container does this — a bucket, a bath, a traffic cone, a wheelie bin. The note depends on how big it is and how wide the opening is.

Big container, low note. Small container, high note. That's really all there is to it.

The trick is matching it up. Get the container's note near your fart's note and it reinforces it, like a free speaker. Get it wrong and the container does nothing at all, and you're just a person sitting on a bucket.

Loud and "hard" are different things

Most people don't actually want a louder fart. They want a harder one. Those aren't the same thing.

Loud is how much sound there is. Hard is how fast it arrives. A sound that hits full volume instantly reads as a crack. The exact same sound faded in over a fifth of a second reads as a rumble. It's all decided in the first few hundredths of a second — everything after that is decoration.

Three things sharpen it up: let go all at once instead of gradually, sit within about 10 cm of something hard, and get that skin-on-leather slap in at the same moment. Your ears glue the slap and the fart into a single event, and the whole thing sounds far more violent than it really was.

Three warnings

Don't strain Holding it in is an acoustic trick, not a workout. Really forcing against a closed valve pushes your blood pressure around and can genuinely hurt you. Six decibels is not worth a hospital trip.
Don't set it on fire The flame can travel back the wrong way. This is the only warning we repeat on every page of this site, and this is the third time you've seen it.
Check which one it is first Never try any of this on a Wet One. Every trick here works by increasing flow and clearing whatever is in the way, and those are the two things you least want in that particular situation.
And obviously All of this works. That's precisely the problem. There's a large gap between can and should, and we're leaving that one entirely with you.
